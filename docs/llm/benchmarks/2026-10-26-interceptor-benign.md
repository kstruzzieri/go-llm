# Interceptor detectors on benign work

This report is the deterministic benign evidence for the detector arm `guards+detectors` (spec §6.2, §6.9, §6.10): the census of committed results by detector rule, carrier and stratum, the unnecessary-block budget on MCP observations per stratum, the D×S interaction, the identity gate on the registered benign task corpus, and the registered power of the deferred model capture. It is generated by `-interceptor-benign-report -registration docs/llm/traces/interceptor-benign/registration.json`, and `TestBenignReportRegen` regenerates it byte for byte. No model ran.

Capture: deferred (K5=b). The model-dependent part (the paired G vs G+D capture on the benign corpus, spec §6.8) runs in the flip PR; see [Capture](#capture).

## Decision

Detectors default: retain opt-in. K2: there is no measured benefit evidence for the detectors until #700's v2 corpus exists, so the benefit gate (spec §7.3) cannot pass in #517. The deterministic harm gate (spec §7.2) fails: the unnecessary-block budget (K1: at most 0.01 of benign MCP observations blocked by guards+detectors, in every stratum) is not met in i18n-cldr-annotations (38/176), i18n-gemoji (1/3) and the pooled i18n stratum (39/179). The deferred capture (K5=b) is a further reason (spec §7.4).

## Power (registered, spec §6.7, §6.8)

Under a true Δ of 0, a non-inferiority contrast over n tasks passes (lower 95% bound of the paired Δ at least −δ) with probability Φ((δ − 1.96·√(q/n)) / √(q/n)), here with assumed q = 0.1 and δ = 0.1. Only the primary contrast gates; a secondary contrast below −δ is reported and named, never failing the gate on its own.
